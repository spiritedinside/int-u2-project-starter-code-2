School Shooting Awareness


School shootings happen. We're all aware of them, but do we really know how bad they are?

Live footage

Live footage not only tells us how these events unfold, but also helps us understand the impact they have on the community. It also helps officers predict how shooters move in an incident such as this.

How it impacts others

The victims and their friends + family are deeply affected by school shootings. The trauma can last for years, impacting their mental health and well-being. It can also impact the broader community of students and educators, who fear an event such as this could unfold at any given moment.

solutions

As the number of school shootings continues to rise, it is crucial that we work together to find effective solutions. Protests and advocacy can help raise awareness and push for policy changes that prioritize student safety.
